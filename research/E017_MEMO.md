# E017 — quadratic DEIM old-tier compression

## Motivation

E007 remains the strongest confirmed canonical result: raw final-layer MSE `2.23e-08`, adjusted `8.17e-09`, mean utilization `0.36666448`, failures `0/100`.

Upstream V21/V24/V25 confines old K3 source legs to a shared basis `Qc` but must re-form dense old `A/P` legs because the D21 algebra uses elementwise products (`A*P`, `P*P`, `A*A`). F86 measures the corresponding V29 families `old_legs + shared = 55.8` units out of `260.1` steady-state units; this is the remaining old-tier wall. E008 tested an oblivious TensorSketch of polynomial products and failed by orders of magnitude. E017 is different: it tests a *data-dependent sampled nonlinear subspace* built from the current `Qc` itself.

## Mechanism

For an old source, `A = Qc FA` and `P = Qc FP`. Every column of the nonlinear products `A*P`, `A*A`, and `P*P` lies in the quadratic feature space generated by pairwise elementwise products of columns of `Qc`.

E017 builds, independently for each MLP/layer, a deterministic quadratic snapshot basis from the current `Qc`:

`Y = (Qc R1) * (Qc R2)`

with fixed Rademacher probe matrices `R1,R2`, no public-data fitting. A rank-192 orthonormal basis `Uq` is obtained from this snapshot matrix. The 256 neuron rows with largest leverage scores of `Uq` are retained. The nonlinear product vector `y` is reconstructed by oversampled gappy-POD/DEIM:

`y_hat = Uq pinv(Uq[I,:]) y[I]`.

A production port would never materialize `y_hat`: it would evaluate the old `A/P` legs only at the 256 selected rows and contract through the precomputed reduced operator. The frozen diagnostic measures the necessary representational condition only.

This is not adaptive-lambda, E012 scheduling/batching, Strassen/HK, MZ/Prony, rank-1 K4, future-aware Qc selection, or E008's oblivious collision sketch.

## Quantitative score path

At 256 sampled rows, the dominant old-leg formation/re-contraction work scales ideally as `256/1024 = 0.25` of its dense-row cost. Applying that only to F86's measured `55.8` units gives gross saving `41.85` units. Reserving a conservative `4.0` units for per-layer quadratic-basis construction/selection leaves net saving `37.85 / 260.1 = 14.5521%`.

Applied conservatively as the same fractional reduction to E007 utilization:

- projected utilization: `0.36666448 * (1 - 0.14552095) = 0.31330712`;
- projected adjusted at unchanged E007 raw: `2.23e-08 * 0.31330712 = 6.98675e-09`;
- at that utilization, raw MSE could rise to `8.17e-09 / 0.31330712 = 2.60767e-08`, a `+16.94%` raw regression, and still equal the E007 adjusted gate.

Therefore the hypothesis has a real score path *if and only if* the sampled nonlinear subspace preserves the old-tier products accurately enough.

## Preregistered gates

All must pass for E017 GO:

1. **Quadratic-product fidelity:** aggregate relative RMS over all frozen validation product samples `<= 0.022` (2.2%).
2. **Tail fidelity:** worst dump/layer/product-family relative RMS `<= 0.05`.
3. **Numerical stability:** every basis/reconstruction finite and `cond(Uq[I,:]) <= 20`.
4. **Projected utilization:** conservative projected utilization `<= 0.32` including the fixed 4-unit basis allowance.
5. **Projected score:** using E007 raw with the conservative projected utilization, adjusted score `< 8.17e-09`.
6. **Scope:** no per-MLP fitted coefficients from reference outputs, no public-data training, no tuning/sweep/holdout/scorer; only online `Qc`-derived state.
7. **Resource shape:** selected-row state is at most one `1024x192` basis, one `256x192` sampled basis/pseudoinverse workspace, and 256 row indices; no persistent per-source dense `n x n` replacement state.

## Kill rule

Any failed gate => `NO-GO / DROP E017`. No rescue under E017 by changing rank, sampled-row count, probe count/seed, validation columns, layer window, or selection rule. A failed structural gate stops before any end-to-end estimator integration or scorer.
