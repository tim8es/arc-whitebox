## New exact identity

(F:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R), let (f) denote its restriction to
(S^{d-1}). On each linear region,

[
\Delta_S f=-(d-1)f.
]

Across each codimension-one activation interface (Gamma), let
(J_Gamma) be the oriented jump of the tangential normal derivative.
Distributionally,

[
\Delta_S f
=
-(d-1)f+
\sum_Gamma J_Gamma\delta_Gamma.
]

Integrating over the closed sphere gives

[
(d-1)\int_{S^{d-1}}f\,d\sigma
=
\sum_Gamma\int_Gamma J_Gamma\,d\sigma_{d-2}.
]

[

=
